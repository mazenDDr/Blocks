Cell culture passaging guide for the fictional cell line HX-3.
Passage HX-3 cells when they reach 80 percent confluence, which usually happens every three days. Wash the cells once with warm phosphate buffered saline, then add trypsin for 4 minutes at 37 degrees Celsius.
Seed the new flask at a density of 20000 cells per square centimeter. Discard the cells after passage number 25 because their growth rate changes.
